About Our School

Learning Today, Leading Tomorrow

SUPS is a professional and student-friendly school located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We provide a balanced learning environment where students grow academically, socially, physically, and emotionally.

Our teaching approach combines strong fundamentals, modern classroom practices, co-curricular activities, and value-based education.
